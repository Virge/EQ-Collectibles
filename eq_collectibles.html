⚔ EQ Collectibles Tracker

📜 How to Use EQ Collectibles Tracker

Overview

This tool reads your EverQuest character achievements export and shows you which collectible items you are still missing, organized by expansion. Load a house storage file to see which of those missing items you already have stashed away and ready to turn in.

In-Game Export Commands

All files are generated by typing these commands in the EverQuest chat window. The output files are saved to your EverQuest folder automatically.

  • /Outputfile achievements — exports your full achievement progress, including collection completion status. Load this with Upload File next to Achievements.
  • /Outputfile realestate — exports a tab-separated list of every item in your in-game house or real estate storage. Load this with Upload Files next to House Files. Run it while inside each house you want to scan.
  • /Outputfile inventory — exports your character's current inventory (bags, bank, etc.) as a tab-separated file. Load this the same way as house files — useful if you keep collectibles in your bank or on your character.

Files You Need

Achievements file — CharacterName_Server-Achievements.txt

Type /Outputfile achievements in-game. The file is saved to your EverQuest folder. Click Upload File next to Achievements and select it.

House / inventory files — real-estate or inventory export, or a plain item list

You have three options for getting items into a house file:

  • Type /Outputfile realestate while inside a house to export everything stored there. The app detects this tab-separated format automatically.
  • Type /Outputfile inventory to export bags, bank, and worn items — handy if collectibles are stashed on your character or in the bank.
  • Take screenshots of your house inventory in-game, paste them into ChatGPT, and ask it to extract the item names into a plain list — one name per line. Save as a .txt file.

You can load multiple files at once. Click Upload Files and select as many as you need. Remove any with the × on its tag.

Tabs

Browse Missing

Select an expansion from the left sidebar to see all collectibles you have not yet obtained, grouped by collection set. If house files are loaded, items already in your house are highlighted with a ♦ and an IN HOUSE badge so you know they are ready to turn in.

vs House (one expansion)

Pick a single expansion from the dropdown to see a focused list of your missing collectibles for that expansion that are present in your house files. Great for targeted turn-ins.

vs House (all expansions)

Same comparison as above but scans every expansion at once and shows a single flat sorted list. Useful when you just want to clear out everything your house has.

All Houses vs All

Combines all loaded house files and matches them against every expansion simultaneously. Adds a House File column so you know exactly which house has each item — handy when collectibles are spread across multiple storage houses.

Tips

  • Item names are clickable links — they open an Allakhazam search in a new tab so you can look up where to find them.
  • Re-export your achievements file from EverQuest after a play session, then reload it with Upload File to refresh without losing your house files.
  • The sidebar shows a count of missing items per expansion. If house files are loaded, the count also shows how many are in your house (♦N).
  • All processing is done locally in your browser — no data is uploaded anywhere.
